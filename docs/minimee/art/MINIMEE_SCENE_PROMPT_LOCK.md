# MiniMee Scene Prompt Lock

## Gameplay master prompt ingredients
Use these concepts for official gameplay-map generation:
- premium 16-bit pixel-art illustration
- old-GTA-style high-angle top-down gameplay camera
- richly decorated but readable
- bright, warm, child-friendly fantasy-world atmosphere
- handcrafted boutique architecture
- dense flowers, foliage, lamps, signage and decorative props
- clear walking routes, entrances and interaction zones
- visually consistent with MiniMee anchor art
- avoid generic tilemap/editor look
- avoid sparse empty environments
- do not move canonical buildings or routes versus Geometry Master

## Night variant
- deep blue evening/night ambience
- warm glowing windows and lamps
- bright enough for children to read the scene
- same geometry, buildings, routes, doors and entrances as Day Master
- magical but not dark/muddy

## Rain / thunderstorm variant
- identical geometry lock
- wet paths, puddles, rain streaks and reflection cues
- umbrellas for walking outdoor characters
- canopies/awnings for stationary outdoor NPCs when appropriate
- preserve route readability and entrance access

## Entrance / loading scene
- 9:16 mobile-first; 16:9 cinematic extension allowed
- same scene identity/landmarks as gameplay map
- suitable for loading/transition
- not authoritative for route geometry
- Day + Night sufficient; weather variants not required

## Interaction / Zoom UI
- integrated pixel-art frame
- child-friendly readable layout
- wood, parchment, stitched-paper and carved-sign details
- decorative but not cluttered
- leave clear programmatic content space
- must look like MiniMee world UI, not a modern generic dashboard

## Guardrails
- Follow the official scene manifest for routes, entrances, doors and interaction placement.
- Reference images are style anchors only; do not copy their route mistakes.
- Keep all Day/Night/Weather versions geometry-identical.
- Preserve gameplay-safe walking width and clear door access.
